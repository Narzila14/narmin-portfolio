Bank Payout Policy: Dividends versus Buybacks

Master's Thesis · 66 Eurozone banks · 64 non-Eurozone banks · fixed-effects panel regression · R Studio

Co-authored with Adam Lamaizi under Prof. Dr. Tamas Vadasz. We tested whether ECB rate hikes pushed listed Eurozone banks to favor share buybacks over dividends, and whether that shift ran through net interest margin (NIM) and capital strength (CET1). The direct link was weak; the real story was in how NIM and CET1 behaved once the tightening period actually began.
