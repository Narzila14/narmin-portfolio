What Makes People Happy in Bulgaria?

Business Research Methods · SPSS · Logistic Regression · Factor Analysis

Group Research Project · KU Leuven

Using data from 1,558 respondents in the 2017 European Values Study, we examined whether life satisfaction, trust, education, age and gender were associated with the likelihood of reporting being happy in Bulgaria.

Model Performance

1,110 observations used in the regression · 70.5% overall classification accuracy · 93.4% sensitivity · ROC AUC = 0.711
